Frequently Asked Questions

What is Benki Finance?

Benki Finance is an AI-powered wealth assistant that helps Africans invest in local and global markets through WhatsApp and Telegram. We make investing simple, secure, and accessible for everyone.

How do I start investing with Benki?

Simply sign up for early access, then chat with our AI assistant on WhatsApp or Telegram. The assistant will guide you through setting up your profile, funding your account, and choosing investments tailored to your goals.

Is Benki Finance regulated?

Yes. We partner only with licensed brokers and regulated funds across Africa. Your investments are protected by leading financial authorities such as SEC Nigeria, CMA Kenya, and FSCA South Africa.

What is the minimum investment amount?

You can start investing with as little as $50 (or local currency equivalent). We believe wealth building should be accessible to everyone.

How do I withdraw my money?

Withdrawals are easy. Just message the AI assistant with your request. Most withdrawals are processed within 1-3 business days, depending on your bank and investment type.

What markets can I invest in?

Benki offers access to major African markets including Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Ghana, and Egypt. We focus on government bonds, equities, and money market funds with strong growth potential.

How does Benki keep my money safe?

Your funds are held in segregated accounts with regulated custodians. We use bank-grade security and comply with all relevant regulations to keep your investments safe.

What fees does Benki charge?

We charge a transparent 0.75% annual management fee on your portfolio value. There are no hidden fees or charges.

Need more help?

Contact us at support@afribenki.com or chat with our AI assistant on WhatsApp or Telegram.
